隐私说明Privacy notes

更新于 2026 年 10 月 8 日Updated 8 October 2026

我们保存什么What we keep

  • 账号：邮箱和加密处理后的密码（不保存明文密码）。Your account: email and a salted hash of your password (never the password itself).
  • 学习记录：你贴进来的文本、生成的讲解和音频、复习卡和复习结果、口语和写作的文字稿与批改、学习时长。Study records: text you paste in, generated explanations and audio, review cards and results, transcripts and corrections of your speaking and writing, and study hours.
  • 内测申请和机构合作表单里填写的内容。What you enter in the beta or partnership forms.

录音Recordings

口语和讲解的录音上传到我们的服务器，由服务器上的语音识别模型转成文字后即丢弃，不保存音频文件，也不发给第三方。部分跟读练习使用浏览器自带的语音识别；这时录音由浏览器厂商（例如 Chrome 由 Google）处理，适用其隐私政策。Speaking and explanation recordings are sent to our server, turned into text by a speech model running there, and then discarded; no audio file is kept or sent to a third party. Some shadowing drills use your browser's built-in speech recognition, in which case the browser vendor (for Chrome, Google) processes the audio under its own policy.

交给第三方处理的内容What third parties process

  • 生成讲解和批改时，相关文本会发送给大语言模型服务（目前主要是 Google Gemini）。To write explanations and corrections, the relevant text is sent to a large language model service (currently mainly Google Gemini).
  • 生成法语音频时，文本会发送给 Microsoft 的语音合成服务。To make French audio, the text is sent to Microsoft's speech synthesis service.

我们不出售你的数据，不投放广告，也不使用广告或跨站追踪。We do not sell your data, show ads or use ad or cross-site tracking.

Cookie 和本地存储Cookies and local storage

登录后使用一个会话 Cookie 保持登录状态。登录凭证、界面语言、所选模型等保存在你自己浏览器的本地存储里。One session cookie keeps you logged in. Your login token and preferences such as interface language and model choice stay in your own browser's local storage.

删除和导出Deleting or exporting

你可以随时要求导出或删除你的账号和全部学习记录。内测期间请通过申请表留言，写明注册邮箱。You can ask at any time for your account and all study records to be exported or deleted. During the beta, leave a message through the form with your account email.
