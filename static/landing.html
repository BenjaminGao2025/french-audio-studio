为什么学了很久，还是开不了口？Why months of study still don't make you speak

看懂了，不等于记住了Understanding is not remembering

读讲解、刷单词表，第二天就忘。记忆是靠“自己想起来”练出来的，不是靠再看一遍。Reading explanations and word lists fades by the next day. Memory grows when you pull an answer out yourself, not when you look at it again.

口语和写作没人批改Nobody corrects your speaking and writing

TCF 的口语和写作由考官人工评分，背下来的模板很容易被看出来。真正缺的是“说出来、写出来，再被纠正”的次数。TCF speaking and writing are scored by people, and memorised templates show. What most learners lack is reps: say it, write it, get corrected.

整块时间太少Too little uninterrupted time

上班、通勤、照顾家人，能坐下来学的时间不多。练习得能拆成十分钟一段，开车时也能听。Work, commuting and family leave little desk time. Practice has to fit into ten-minute pieces, and into the drive.

怎么练：先回想，再看答案How it works: recall first, then check

  1. 1

    拆：用你自己的材料Break down your own material

    把课文、TCF 样题、新闻贴进来，按句子和意群拆开：逐词对照、语法、发音，每句都能播放和跟读。Paste in lessons, TCF samples or news. Each sentence is split into chunks with a word-by-word gloss, grammar and pronunciation, and you can play and shadow it.

  2. 2

    练：听、说、填三种卡Drill: hear, say, fill

    学过的句子变成复习卡。先听后说，答案要自己打出来或说出来，系统按你的表现安排下次复习。Sentences you study become review cards. You type or say every answer yourself, and the next review is scheduled by how you did.

  3. 3

    讲：费曼式自测Explain it back

    用中文把一句话讲给 AI 听。AI 不先讲，只告诉你讲对了什么、漏了什么，再追问一个问题。Explain a sentence to the AI in your own words. It never explains first: it tells you what you got right, what you missed, and asks one follow-up question.

  4. 4

    用：按 TCF 题型输出Use it: TCF-style output

    口语按 TCF 三个任务练，写作按考试题型写，AI 逐句指出问题并给出修改。Practise the three TCF speaking tasks and the writing tasks; the AI marks problems sentence by sentence and suggests fixes.

和多邻国、背单词 App 有什么不同？How is this different from Duolingo or flashcard apps?

练的是整句和你自己的材料，重点在说和写的输出，并且按 TCF 题型练，而不是选择题和单词。You drill whole sentences from your own material, with the weight on speaking and writing in TCF task formats, not multiple choice and single words.
